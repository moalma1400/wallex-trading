## Scope and counting rules

- `discovered` counts URL destinations explicitly enumerated in the retrieved developer sitemap (one record per exact page URL). The sitemap document itself is discovery metadata, not included in the 115 page count.
- `reviewed` means page content was returned by the research reader and inspected for this pass. It does not mean every endpoint or every statement on a large page was exhaustively reconciled.
- `not_started` is a known page-level gap, not an inaccessible page. `blocked` is used for actual observed retrieval failures; causes are not guessed.
- Exact URLs are retained. No guessed route was silently substituted for a broken link.
- Source register structural validation is offline and does not test remote link availability. Internal Markdown links are checked separately; external network links are never rewritten.
- Coverage will expand beyond sitemap when official navigation or verified Wallex links reveal additional in-scope pages. The current snapshot does not establish exhaustiveness of the whole support center/marketing site.
