# Real-exchange compliance gate procedure

1. Keep current execution disabled. Do not use an account or API while conducting this review.
2. Identify the exact proposed activity, entity/person, place of establishment/residence, counterparties, services, data flows, payment rails, and relevant dates. Do not infer jurisdiction from IP, location hints, or citizenship alone.
3. Collect current primary legal/regulatory/sanctions sources for each applicable jurisdiction. Record exact URLs, issuing authority, publication/update/effective dates, and what remains uncertain. Distinguish official law from commentary and exchange marketing.
4. Include sanctions designation, prohibited dealings, geo/access restrictions, licensing, consumer/financial rules, tax, privacy/data, banking/payment, and the exchange's own eligibility/terms as questions for qualified professionals. Never suggest bypassing identity, sanctions, access, or anti-abuse controls.
5. Require independent, qualified legal/compliance review and explicit owner approval before considering any separate technical proposal. Do not state the gate is passed on the basis of public API docs, tests, or a model's interpretation.
6. Document that review is time-sensitive and no legality or account eligibility is guaranteed. Do not provide individualized legal advice.
